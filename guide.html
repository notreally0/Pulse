OPERATIONAL INSTRUCTIONS

1. HOW TO PLAY SOUNDS

You can trigger the synthesizer frequencies using two methods:

  • Keyboard Hotkeys: Press keys [A], [S], [D], [F], [G], or [H] on your physical keyboard.
  • Mouse Click: Click directly on any of these neon synth pad buttons inside the system.

2. PATTERN RECORDER

Record your custom synth loops directly in the browser:

  • REC: Click to start recording. Play keys in your desired timing rhythm. Click again to stop.
  • PLAY: Play back your recorded synth pattern with accurate timing.
  • CLEAR: Wipe the current recorded buffer to start fresh.
